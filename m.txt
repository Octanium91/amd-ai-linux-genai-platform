Videos can get a soundtrack

A video job may carry an audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A or a video
with sound) with a start point and an optional fade-out, or, for "Change a
video", the sound of the uploaded video. The worker muxes it into the
finished clip, cut or padded with silence to its exact length; if that
fails the clip is kept silent with a warning. WORKER_API 3.
